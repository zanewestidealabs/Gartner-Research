Purpose

Client Utility - Markets and Vendors

Market Insights is designed as a short-form content piece, making it easy to consume and understand. The concise yet informative segments provide valuable context, enriching clients’ overall market understanding enabling quicker, more informed decision-making.

Market Insights examine impacts and implications about the market or technology under examination. They  include a conclusion section to discuss what the affected vendors or Product Leaders should do about what you've told them.

What You Need to Know

●       Only used when analysis does not fit into the more defined High Tech and Invest (HTI)​  role-based content types: such as Data View - Detailed, First 100 Days, Impact Appraisal, Market Opportunity Map or Top Practices.

●       Clearly identify impact/ implications for HTIs or Product Leaders.

●       Analysis should be molded into a single argument using a simple, clear structure that clearly identifies the audience.

How to Write, Structure and Style a Market Insight

The Section Heading column specifies whether each section is required or optional and indicates whether or not the heading in the CPP canvas will be published on g.com.

Section Heading

How to Write, Structure and Style It

Title

Required

Header won’t publish

No set structure, but there are three suggested structures, based on your use.

●       For technology analysis:

○       “Technology Insight: [technology name], [period]”

○       Example: Technology Insight: Generative AI, 2Q24

●       For industry analysis:

○       “[Industry] Insight: [description]”

○       Example: Insurance Insight: How to Position and Differentiate CDN and Digital Enablement Services

●       For role-focused analysis:

○       [Role] Insight: [description]

○       Example: Product Manager Insight: Leverage Application Software Ecosystems to Deliver Rapid Innovation

●       Ideal Length: 20 to 65 characters (including spaces)

Summary

Required

Header won’t publish

Contextualize your research in 2-3 sentences, using key search terms where possible.

●       In 2-3 sentences, convey the specific challenge, opportunity or situation that your research addresses. Then, outline how this research will help the approved target role.

●       Length: 300 characters (including spaces) HARD LIMIT

Executive Summary

Required
Header won’t publish

Executive Summary helps clients quickly understand the note's relevance.

●       This section consists of two subsections: Key Findings, Recommendation(s).

●       Ideal Length: Maximum 300 words.

Key Findings:

●       Include a bulleted list of 2 to 4 insights that the client can find in this piece of research.

●       Present these as the main challenge(s), business issue(s), common mistakes, opportunities, trends, survey data results or something else relevant to your piece.

Recommendation(s):

●       Relate the findings and recommendations to the end-user client. What is challenging about making decisions in this market? What actions should end users take given the dynamics of the market, and benefits and risks it poses?

●       Directly address the client problem/opportunity/situation identified in the summary.

●       Be specific and action-oriented. Avoid platitudes.

●       Ideal Limit: 4 bullet points

Strategic Planning Assumption(s)

Optional

●       Enter relevant SPAs here if your research team uses them. For guidance on using SPAs, see The Art of Writing Good SPAs/Predictions.

Analysis

Required

Organize the content into a single argument using a simple, clear structure that clearly identifies the audience.

●       Ideally, add two to four subheads to break up the text and allow the client to navigate the document easily.

●       If appropriate, use figures and charts to help explain and present your analysis.

Background and Context

Optional

Profile and describe the market, technology, competitor, customer or (HTI)​ strategies discussed in this research.

●       Ideal Length: Up to 500 words

The Impact
Optional

●       Identify the implications/impact for your audience. Specify how you expect these effects to be manifested and when you expect them to occur (now, next year or five years from now).

●       Explain why these manifestations are important for your audience — do they create new opportunities, new threats or a new customer buying center?

●       Be sure to specify “who” is affected (vendor types, regions, countries, verticals and roles/functions).

●       Ideal Length: Up to 2,000 words

Conclusion

Optional

Discuss what your audience should do about what you've told them. For example:

●       What parts of their strategy or business model they should reassess — product, competition, go-to-market — to be able to grow in light of the key findings and takeaways.

●       What things they should think about/assess in the short term as a result of your analysis (this year and next year) versus the long term (five years out).

●       Ideal Length: Up to 500 words

Contributors

Optional

 

Use this space to acknowledge associates who provide meaningful input, advice and/or raw material in the creation of research content but who are not co-authors/co-creators.

Required

●       Recognize associates who contributed but are not co-authors.

●       Format: first name + last name; list alphabetically by last name, separated by commas. Only include associate names. HARD RULE

●       Ensure correct spelling; no nicknames.

Acronym Key and Glossary Terms

Optional

●       Include market and technology definitions, if needed.

●       Refer to this link to see Gartner’s standard definitions Glossary

Evidence

Optional

Use the evidence section to provide relevant background for the entire document.

●       No Market Insight document should be written without first doing secondary research to see what has been written elsewhere in Gartner and what is available externally.

●       Share what research and/or expertise qualifies Gartner to address your topic. For example, internal Gartner evidence, external sources, vendor frameworks, surveys, case studies, anecdotes, inquiries and interviews you conducted for this note.

●       If you find nothing specific to your note other than analyst experience, some examples of how to accomplish this are statements like:

○       “Based on ongoing multiple interactions with leading HTTP vendors ...”

○       “Based on secondary research and interactions with XXX ...”

○       “The advice provided in this document is built from constant scanning of the market, and from the aggregation of analysts' experience and interactions with vendors.”

Notes

Optional

●       Use notes to add non-essential detail or context

●       Replace the text “Notes” with this format: “Note X: [Title].”

Instructions to Editor

Market Insights is a short-form document type that provides background information about a market or technology. It is primarily used when analysis does not fit into more-defined High Tech and Invest (HTI) ​role-based content types.

Your editorial responsibilities for this document type:

●       The Summary must not exceed 300 characters (including spaces). The role should be stated in the Summary.

●       If the contributor’s section is present, only names should be included (no additional details, nicknames or team names). Format: [first name] [last name]; list alphabetically by last name, separated by commas.

●       Case-in-points and downloads are not permitted in this content type.

●       Reference the supplemental Authoring Kits for all Emerging Tech and Trends (ETT) content.
