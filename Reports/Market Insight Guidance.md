How to Write, Structure and Style a Market Insight
The Section Heading column specifies whether each section is required or optional and indicates whether or not the
heading in the CPP canvas will be published on g.com.
Section Heading How to Write, Structure and Style It
Title No set structure, but there are three suggested structures, based on your use.
Required ● For technology analysis:
Header won’t publish ○ “Technology Insight: [technology name], [period]”
○ Example: Technology Insight: Generative AI, 2Q24
● For industry analysis:
○ “[Industry] Insight: [description]”
○ Example: Insurance Insight: How to Position and Differentiate CDN and
Digital Enablement Services
● For role-focused analysis:
○ [Role] Insight: [description]
○ Example: Product Manager Insight: Leverage Application Software
Ecosystems to Deliver Rapid Innovation
● Ideal Length: 20 to 65 characters (including spaces)
Summary Contextualize your research in 2-3 sentences, using key search terms where possible.
Required
● In 2-3 sentences, convey the specific challenge, opportunity or situation that your
Header won’t publish
research addresses. Then, outline how this research will help the approved target
role.
● Length: 300 characters (including spaces) HARD LIMIT
Executive Summary Executive Summary helps clients quickly understand the note's relevance.
Required ● This section consists of two subsections: Key Findings, Recommendation(s).
Header won’t publish ● Ideal Length: Maximum 300 words.
Key Findings:
● Include a bulleted list of 2 to 4 insights that the client can find in this piece of
research.
● Present these as the main challenge(s), business issue(s), common mistakes,
opportunities, trends, survey data results or something else relevant to your piece.
Recommendation(s):
● Relate the findings and recommendations to the end-user client. What is
challenging about making decisions in this market? What actions should end
users take given the dynamics of the market, and benefits and risks it poses?
● Directly address the client problem/opportunity/situation identified in the summary.
● Be specific and action-oriented. Avoid platitudes.
● Ideal Limit: 4 bullet points

Instructions to Editor
Market Insights is a short-form document type that provides background information about a market or technology. It
is primarily used when analysis does not fit into more-defined High Tech and Invest (HTI) role-based content types.
Your editorial responsibilities for this document type:
● The Summary must not exceed 300 characters (including spaces). The role should be stated in the Summary.
● If the contributor’s section is present, only names should be included (no additional details, nicknames or
team names). Format: [first name] [last name]; list alphabetically by last name, separated by commas.
● Case-in-points and downloads are not permitted in this content type.
● Reference the supplemental Authoring Kits for all Emerging Tech and Trends (ETT) content.
