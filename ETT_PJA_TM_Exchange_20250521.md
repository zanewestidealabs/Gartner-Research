POSITION
Components of a Powerful Position Statement

Powerful positions are declarative statements providing
Gartner judgments on the current or future state or
consequences of important issues that matter to our clients’
success in their roles.
The primary goals of a position statement are to:
• Command reader attention
• Surface an unavoidable and irresistible challenge or opportunity
• Motivate the desire to act

JUSTIFICATION
Components of a Well-Presented Justification
 The justification paragraphs explain the situation and its importance
(context).
 They provide evidence and logic that establishes validity of your position.
 It sets up the reasoning for why action is necessary.
Sources of evidence:
• Data-driven fact-base such as VC investment data or primary research survey data
• Real-world case studies such as vendor examples, peer reviews, inquiry and implementation
case-based research.
• Prior art source references such as academic papers, Gartner published research, and articles
from reputable and trusted sources.
Bottom Line: The justification must convince the reader that your position(s) are valid and that
they merit action.

ACTIONS
Actions are How We Recommend Clients Respond
 Recommended actions list out how we believe clients should respond to our
stated positions to achieve success in their roles.
How Clients Should Respond to Our Positions
Key Characteristics of Actions:
✓ Cover “how to” level actions that address the challenge or opportunity in the position statements.
✓ Are non-obvious and meaningful in impact.
✓ Include the results achieved if actions are successfully implemented
Bottom Line: Recommended actions should provide previously unknown or little-known remedies
to the challenge\opportunity outlined in the position statement.

Help make the recommended actions specific with a
