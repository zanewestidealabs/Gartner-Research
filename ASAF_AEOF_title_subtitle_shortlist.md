# ASAF and AEOF Title & Subtitle Shortlist

**Updated:** June 8, 2026  
**Purpose:** Provide strong naming options for both ebooks, including title/subtitle packages, tonal notes, positioning implications, and recommended frontrunners.

---

## Naming principles

A strong title package for these books should do four things:

1. signal that the book is about an operating-model shift, not generic AI enthusiasm
2. feel serious enough for an executive or strategist audience
3. carry enough conceptual sharpness to stand as a flagship framework book
4. avoid sounding like a vendor white paper or consulting leave-behind

---

## ASAF title shortlist

### What the ASAF title should communicate

The ASAF book is the sharper, more thesis-driven of the two. Its title should communicate at least one of the following:

- the end of the tiered SOC
- the rise of agentic security operations
- the idea of architectural substitution
- the move from alert queues to a governed control system

| Rank | Title | Subtitle | Why it works | Tone |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **The End of the Tiered SOC** | How security operations becomes a governed control system | Clear problem statement, strong tension, memorable | Sharp / thesis-driven |
| 2 | **The Agentic SOC** | A framework for the post-analyst operating model | Compact, category-defining, ownable | Modern / flagship |
| 3 | **Agentic Security Operations** | Designing the shift from alert queues to governed autonomy | Broad but precise and strong for executive readers | Strategic / formal |
| 4 | **From Tiered Defense to Governed Autonomous Security Operations** | A new operating architecture for the enterprise SOC | Highly descriptive and serious | Formal / explanatory |
| 5 | **Beyond Alerts and Escalations** | Rebuilding security operations as a cybernetic control system | Distinctive and thought-leadership friendly | Conceptual / provocative |

### Secondary ASAF candidates

- **The Cybernetic SOC** — _A new architecture for governed autonomous security operations_
- **After the Alert Queue** — _The future of security operations beyond triage and escalation_

### Recommended ASAF frontrunners

## Paired-book naming logic

Because the books are related, their titles should feel aligned but not overly matched.

### Best paired set: thesis-first

- **ASAF:** _The End of the Tiered SOC_
- **AEOF:** _Beyond Queues and Escalations_

Why it works:

- each title names the old model being left behind
- both feel thesis-driven and contemporary
- they are different enough to avoid sounding like volume labels

### Best paired set: framework-brand family

- **ASAF:** _The Agentic SOC_
- **AEOF:** _Agentic Enterprise Operations_

Why it works:

- strong reusable framework branding
- clean, professional, and scalable to workshops and derivative assets

### Best paired set: executive-safe family

- **ASAF:** _Agentic Security Operations_
- **AEOF:** _The Agentic Operations Model_

Why it works:

- formal, serious, and easy to explain in enterprise contexts
