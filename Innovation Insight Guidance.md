Coverage Evolution - Selecting the Appropriate Content Type
● Trigger - A new technology, product, service, capability or market goes through a very early embryonic stage.
One primary source of initial Gartner coverage of an innovation is a “dot” (Innovation Profile) on a Hype Cycle.
Other sources could be Impact Radars (HTI only) or technology profiles in a Trends Explorer.
● Insight - The next step is to expand on the initial coverage by providing additional insight and explanation
using an Innovation Insight.
● Progression - Once the technology, product, service, capability or market shifts from nascent to emerging,
the analyst should now utilize one of the following doc types [methodology to provide guidance here].

Appendix: Incorporating Case in Point
Case content is a proven practical, real-world application of a technology, process or initiative by a single named or
anonymous company to solve their own internal business problem.
Clients consume case content in two ways: as a case study and as a Case in Point. A Case in Point is:
● A short, real-world example to support a broader teaching, obtained from either:
○ A full written case study, summarized for a different context, or
○ Secondary, reputable sources
● A component of a larger research note or library. Unlike a full case study, it does not stand alone in the
client experience.
Cases in Point do not have a defined structure. They are components inserted into non-case-study content types,
such as Top Practices or Impact Appraisals.
For more details, see the Case in Point guidelines.
