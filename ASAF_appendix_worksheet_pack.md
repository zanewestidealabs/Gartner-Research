# ASAF Appendix Worksheet Pack

**Purpose:** Provide the first working appendix tools for the ASAF ebook in a fillable Markdown format.

This pack includes:

1. an ASAF stage-assessment worksheet
2. an ASAF vendor-category diagnostic checklist
3. a short buyer evaluation prompt set

---

## How to use this pack

These worksheets are meant to be practical tools, not passive back matter.

Use them to:

- assess a current-state program
- structure a workshop discussion
- compare vendors against the ASAF model
- identify where a platform contributes to stage advancement
- separate architectural relevance from feature abundance

## Worksheet 2 — ASAF vendor-category diagnostic checklist

### Instructions

Use this checklist to classify a vendor or platform as one of the following:

- **Enabler**
- **Neutral**
- **Displaced**

For each prompt, mark one of these answers:

- **Yes**
- **Partly**
- **No**

Then add supporting evidence.

### Diagnostic prompts

Record an answer and evidence for each item below.

1. Does the product contribute materially to Stage 2+ advancement?
2. Does it operate credibly in at least one ASAF plane?
3. Does it contribute signals or structured outputs to adjacent planes?
4. Does it write structured evidence back to a shared model or graph?
5. Does it strengthen governance, authority control, or auditability?
6. Does it reduce dependence on analyst-tier queue processing?
7. Does its value increase as buyers move from Stage 1 to Stage 3?
8. Would the architecture become weaker if this product were removed?
9. Is the product still described mainly through alert triage, queue efficiency, or playbook execution?
10. Does the product depend on unresolved Stage 0 to Stage 1 labor problems to remain central?

### Classification guide

Use the checklist qualitatively, but this rule of thumb helps:

- **Enabler**
  - strong positive evidence in stage advancement, plane participation, governance or graph contribution, and increasing value at higher maturity
- **Neutral**
  - some useful integration or signal contribution, but limited evidence of structural stage-advancement value
- **Displaced**
  - product remains centered on alert triage, manual queue optimization, or problems that shrink as Stage 2+ architectures mature

## Worksheet 3 — Buyer evaluation prompts

### Instructions

Use these prompts in vendor briefings, architecture reviews, or internal roadmap discussions.

### Architecture fit prompts

- Which ASAF plane does this product actually operate in?
- What signal does it contribute to adjacent planes?
- What happens in the architecture if this product is removed?
- Does the product strengthen the loop, or merely consume from it?

### Governance prompts

- How is authority modeled in the system?
- What happens when confidence falls below the threshold for autonomous action?
- How are audit chains produced and preserved?
- What policy objects or governance artifacts does the system read or write?

### Maturity prompts

- At what ASAF stage does this product become strategically more valuable?
- At what stage does its current positioning start to weaken?
- What specific evidence shows readiness for Stage 2 or Stage 3 behavior?
- Which ASAF dimension does the product improve most credibly?

### Replacement-risk prompts

- Is this product solving a real architectural problem or a decaying queue-era problem?
- Does the product become more central as L1 and L2 work disappear, or less?
- Is the product a contributor to the shared intelligence spine, or a consumer of it?
- Would a more integrated platform absorb this function over time?
